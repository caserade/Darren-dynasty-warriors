CLAUDE UPDATE 2 · THE 3D GAME BECOMES A REAL GAME · 2026-10-12
Built on update 1. The pixel game is unchanged; every rule below copies the pixel game
(dist/game.js) where it has one, converted from pixels to metres. Where the pixel game
doesn't decide something, the choice made here is marked DEFAULT.

ENEMIES (dist/3d/sim.mjs GRUNTS)
- Gophers (22 HP, hit 5), crows (14 HP, hit 4, fast and wobbly) and hay bales (46 HP,
  hit 8, slow), same numbers as the pixel game. Each one winds up for about half a
  second before it hits, and at most three wind up at once.
- Ruffy chapter: small groups wander in 18-26 m away. More come as you find clues
  (6 at a time, up to 12). DEFAULT: none ever appear within 22 m of Mom's cottage, so
  the practice posts stay a safe place; only 3 at a time during a boss fight.
- Firewood chapter: no wandering groups. Each wood pile has 3, 4 and 5 guards (like the
  pixel game) that appear when you get close, and Bucky can't gather until they're gone.
- Defeated enemies tumble away. 7% drop a cookie and 13% drop a blue energy orb
  (DEFAULT: orbs refill 30 energy, since the 3D game has energy instead of the
  pixel game's Blizzard meter).
- Grunts get a small health bar once hit. Damage numbers float up from every hit.

BOSSES (BOSSES, updateBoss)
- Sergeant Spudsworth (440 HP: swipe, slam) guards Spud Barricade, Madame Tumbleweed
  (540: charge, swipe) guards Windswept Ring, Colonel Cluck (640: slam, egg volley,
  swipe) guards Coop Command, and the Bramble Baron (1350: slam, thorn ring, charge,
  swipe; hits 30% harder) appears in Bramble Hollow after the third clue, with 6 guards.
  They're built from simple shapes, like the practice posts.
- Each boss says the pixel game's opening lines when you get close. A boss bar shows
  its health and a tip. Every attack shows a red warning on the ground first (an arc
  for a swipe, a circle for a slam, a lane for a charge) for 0.6-1 second.
- Beating a boss clears the camp: friends are healed by 25 and revived, nearby grunts
  are knocked out, and 2 cookies and an orb drop. The camp's clue can then be picked
  up with Interact, which still gives the same dialogue as before.
- Clues now need their boss beaten first, and Ruffy needs the Baron beaten.
- DEFAULT: if you run more than 45 m away, the boss goes home and heals. If a boss is
  stuck on a ledge it can't get down from for 5 seconds, it hops back to its camp.

HEALTH, KNOCK-OUTS AND DEFEAT
- Each hero has 100 health, shown in the hero panel and as a thin bar under each name
  in the party buttons. Getting hit flashes the screen edge and shakes the camera.
- Dodging makes you untouchable for the first 0.45 s. After a hit you're untouchable
  for 0.7 s (pixel game value).
- DEFAULT: friends you aren't playing slowly heal (1 per second).
- When the hero you're playing is knocked out, the next friend steps in. A resting
  friend can't be picked until a cookie or a cleared camp revives them.
- When all three are knocked out you get the pixel game's "A little rest. Another try."
  screen. Try again starts beside the last cleared camp (or the cottage) with everyone
  healed. Cleared camps, clues and cookies are kept. Return to the cottage also works.
- Return to the cottage from Pause now also heals everyone.
- New Mode setting in Pause: Adventure, or Cozy, where every hit does 55% damage and
  cookies heal 32 instead of 22 (pixel game values).

FRIENDS FIGHT TOO
- DEFAULT: friends you aren't playing swing at enemies right beside them
  (Darren 16, Tormund 18 on one enemy, Bucky 26 to everything around him), with the
  same 1.4-1.8 s rhythm as the pixel game's companions.

HOW MUCH THE HEROES HIT FOR
- Combo hits: Darren 16, 19, 28 (the pixel game's Bare Paws jab, cross and kick);
  DEFAULT Tormund 18, 20, 30 and Bucky 20, 22, 34.
- Charged attacks: Darren's packed snowball 30-70 and it rolls through grunts;
  Tormund's cleave 28-62; Bucky's quake 26-56 all around him.
- K.O. counter and combo counter at the top right. Every 100 K.O.s heals 15.

COOKIES NOW MATTER
- Each cookie heals the hero you're playing by 22 (32 in Cozy), heals the other two by
  half that, and gets a resting friend back up.
- Every 3 cookies grow Darren one belly stage, with the pixel game's names: Little
  adventurer, Cozy belly, Round & ready, Snowball sturdy. Each stage adds 4 to his
  combo finisher and 5 to his packed snowball (+12 and +15 at the top stage, like the
  pixel game). The hero panel shows the stage and progress, and Darren's model gets a
  little rounder (a gentle width scale only; the model, horn and torso are untouched).
- The 6 hidden cookies are still there; bosses and enemies drop more. The quest panel
  shows the total.

SAVES
- Still the snowday-3d-v1 save, version 1, with new optional fields: mode, bosses,
  ko, bestCombo, lastCamp. Cookies can now go past 6. Older saves still load: any
  clue already found counts as that camp's boss beaten, so nobody has to refight.
- The pixel game's saves and the 3D controller buttons are untouched.

SCREEN CHANGES
- Pause menu note, help text and quest text now describe the fights.
- New: boss bar, K.O. chip, health bars, belly stage line, big banners (boss names,
  camps cleared, belly stages), red hurt edge, damage numbers, the rest screen.
- The rest screen works with keyboard (Enter), mouse and controller (D-pad and A).
- Pressing 1-3 or Next hero skips a resting friend and says why.

TESTS
- tests/three-d.mjs: 27 checks (was 14). The old "no enemies exist" check was removed
  because enemies now exist. The full-quest walk runs with enemies off, and a new check
  plays the whole game with them on: all four bosses, Ruffy home, then the firewood
  chapter with its guards.
- tests/browser-qa.cjs has a new "3d fight" section (grunts, boss lines, warnings,
  belly stage, clearing a camp, defeat and Try again, Cozy mode across a reload, and
  phone layouts with the boss bar).

LEFT FOR LATER
- Darren's Blizzard special, the Bare Paws / Snowball style choice and the tail sweep
  from the pixel game aren't in 3D. They need new animation work.
- The bosses are simple-shape models with no animation clips; they bob, squash and

CLAUDE UPDATE 3 · TIDY CODE, SHARED SAVES, GIT · 2026-10-12

READABLE CODE (no behaviour change)
- All game scripts, styles and pages were squeezed onto very long lines (some over 700
  characters). They are now laid out one statement per line with Prettier:
  dist/*.js, dist/3d/*.mjs, every .css and .html file, tests/ and tools/, and
  desktop-server.cjs. Vendor libraries and the big generated art files were left alone.
- Prettier's own check confirmed every file parses to exactly the same program before
  and after. The settings are in .prettierrc.json; `npm run format` repeats it.
- desktop-server.py and tools/pack-firewood.py were laid out the same way by hand / Black.
- Save keys and formats are unchanged: darren-snowday-v3, darren-firewood-v1,
  darren-controls-v1, darren-look-v1, snowday-3d-v1, snowday-3d-controls-v1.

SHARED BETWEEN BOTH GAMES (new file dist/shared-save.js)
- Both games load it. Each game keeps its own save exactly as before; one new small
  record, snowday-shared-v1, holds what means the same in both games.
- Sound: turning sound off (pixel music button, 3D Sound box) carries to the other game.
- Mode: the Adventure / Cozy choice you made last becomes the starting mode of a brand-new
  3D adventure, and the pixel game's character cards mark it "last pick". An existing 3D
  save keeps its own mode.
- Controller buttons: Attack (pixel Throw = 3D Combo), Dodge and Pause carry across when
  you remap them on purpose. A button already in use swaps, the same way remapping does.
  "Restore standard buttons" in either game forgets the shared choice. Other buttons
  differ between the games and stay separate.
- Story: each title screen shows one line about the other game ("In the pixel game:
  Ruffy is home, 2 of 3 wood piles gathered."). This is read straight from the other
  game's save, so old saves count at once and nothing is copied.
- Rescuing Ruffy in the pixel game opens Firewood for Mom in the 3D game. If you start it
  before finishing the 3D rescue, the same Pause button then says "Back to Ruffy's
  rescue" and your camps, clues and wood are all kept.
- Not shared on purpose: cookies and Darren's belly size, health, K.O.s and camp
  progress. Each adventure grows Darren from little during that run, and the pixel
  game's camp checkpoint rules (run token, matching camps) would break if another game
  wrote into it. Scarf, breathing wobble and growth on/off exist only in the pixel game.
- Both local servers (desktop-server.cjs and .py) now reuse a copy of the game that is
  already running instead of starting a second one on the next port. Browser saves
  belong to one address, so before this, opening PLAY-PIXEL while PLAY-3D was running put
  the two games on different ports with separate saves.

GIT
- Added README.md, .gitattributes (Windows launchers keep CRLF; art is binary) and a
  `format` script in package.json. node_modules and .DS_Store are ignored.
- EXPORT-MANIFEST.json and EXPORT-VALIDATION.txt describe the original ChatGPT export and
  were already out of date after updates 1 and 2; they are kept as a historical record.

TESTS
- New tests/shared-save.cjs (8 checks), now part of npm test.
- tests/three-d.mjs: new check that firewood started early can switch back to the rescue
  without losing anything (28 checks).
- tests/browser-qa.cjs: new "shared" section driving both games in one browser.

LEFT FOR LATER
- Enemy and boss numbers are still written in two places (dist/game.js in pixels,
  dist/3d/sim.mjs in metres). Merging them would mean loading one game's code into the
  other and re-checking all the balance, so it was left as is.
- For update 4: the 3D models are still 17 MB, and there are still only Windows launchers.

CLAUDE UPDATE 4 · SMALLER 3D MODELS, MAC LAUNCHERS · 2026-10-12

SMALLER 3D MODELS (dist/3d/assets, 17.0 MB -> 6.3 MB)
- tormund.glb 7.60 -> 2.63 MB, bucky.glb 5.65 -> 1.72 MB, terrain.glb 2.21 -> 1.29 MB,
  darren.glb 0.93 -> 0.65 MB.
- Most of the size was Tormund's and Bucky's bumpiness (normal) and shininess
  (metal/roughness) maps, stored as 2048x2048 PNGs. They are now
  2048x2048 JPEGs at quality 90 with no colour subsampling, the same format the colour
  textures already used. Texture size, colour textures, animations, bone names and
  materials are unchanged.
- Vertex positions, normals, texture coordinates and bone weights are stored in smaller
  number types (the standard KHR_mesh_quantization option, which Three.js reads with no
  extra decoder file). Positions move by less than 0.1 mm on the characters and about
  2 mm on the 144 m terrain.
- Duplicate data (one repeated skin per character, unused accessors) was removed.
- Checked by rendering every model before and after from several angles, close up and
  mid-attack: the pictures differ only on a few edge pixels (0.01-0.5% of pixels), and a
  Tormund close-up side by side shows no visible change. Triangle counts and bounding
  boxes match.
- tools/shrink-models.mjs is the script that did it (gltf-transform + sharp, installed
  outside the folder with --no-save; see the comment at its top). The original models are
  in the first git commit and in the original download.
- Not used on purpose: WebP/KTX2 textures and Draco/meshopt compression. They would shrink
  the models further but need newer browsers or extra decoder files shipped with the
  game; JPEG and quantization work everywhere the game already ran.

MAC LAUNCHERS
- New PLAY-3D.command and PLAY-PIXEL.command: double-click in Finder. Like the Windows
  .cmd launchers they use Node.js if installed, otherwise Python 3, and say what to install
  if neither is there. They also look in /opt/homebrew/bin and /usr/local/bin, where the
  Node.js and Homebrew installers put node.
- desktop-server.cjs now opens the browser on a Mac too (it only did on Windows);
  desktop-server.py already did. Opening the second launcher while the first runs reuses
  the same address, so saves stay shared, as in update 3.
- .gitattributes keeps the .command files with Unix line endings, and git marks them
  executable.
- First run on a Mac: macOS may refuse a launcher downloaded from the internet. Right-click
  it, choose Open, then Open again; after that a double-click works. If it says you lack
  "access privileges" (the zip lost the executable mark), open Terminal and run
  `chmod +x ` followed by dragging the launcher into the window, then press Return.
- README.md and START-HERE.txt describe both.

CLAUDE UPDATE 5 · WINDOWS DESKTOP APP · 2026-10-12

WHAT'S NEW
- A Windows app: Darren-Snowday-Windows.zip. Unzip it and double-click
  "Darren Snowday.exe". It needs nothing else installed (no Node.js, Python or browser);
  it brings its own copy of Chromium through Electron 44.4.5.
- One window plays both games. It opens on a start screen with Darren's art and two
  choices, Pixel adventure and 3D adventure (mouse, keys 1/2, or a controller's
  left/right and A). The games' own links between each other still work.
- Menu: Game > Choose a game (Ctrl+H), Pixel adventure (Ctrl+1), 3D adventure (Ctrl+2),
  Quit; View > Full screen (F11), Reload, zoom; Test > Developer tools, Open save folder.
- Both games are served from one fixed address inside the app (snowday://game/), so
  they share saves exactly as with the launchers, and saves stay between runs. They are
  stored in %APPDATA%\Darren's Snowday Search, separate from browser saves.
- Only one copy of the app runs at a time (a second double-click brings the first window
  forward), so two copies never write the same saves. Web links open in the normal browser.
- The .exe has Darren's icon and its own name and details. It is not code-signed, so
  Windows SmartScreen warns the first time ("More info", then "Run anyway").

FILES
- desktop-app/: main.cjs (window, menu, file serving), home/ (start screen and icon),
  icon.ico, build-windows.cjs (npm run build:win), package.json and package-lock.json,
  README.md. Builds go to desktop-app/out/ (ignored by git).
- tests/desktop-app.cjs: launches the app and checks it (not part of npm test).
- The games in dist/ are unchanged; the build copies them into the app.
- The PLAY-* launchers still work as before.

CLAUDE UPDATE 6 · FLUID ANIMATION, CHARACTER COMBOS, POLKA SCARF, SMARTER AI · 2026-10-12
3D game only (dist/3d/). The pixel game is untouched, at michael's request. (Update 5,
the Windows app, lives on its own branch / PR #1.) Scope and before-pictures:
/mnt/project-files/animation-pass/scope.md in the project files.

HOW THE NEW ANIMATION WORKS
- The supplied models, skins, textures and Darren's look are unchanged. New motion is
  authored in code as key poses on the existing skeletons (dist/3d/clips.mjs) and turned
  into Three.js animation clips when the game loads (dist/3d/anim.mjs). Authored clips
  replace the supplied ones of the same name; nothing went back through Blender.
- New animator (anim.mjs Animator) instead of the flat 0.12 s cross-fades: per-transition
  blend times, walk/run blended and speed-matched to real ground speed (feet don't
  slide), turn-rate-limited facing with lean and banking, head look-at toward the fight,
  spring tails and wings, landing squash, hit flinches, and hit-stop on heavy hits.
- Movement has momentum (smoothed acceleration and turning). Jumping is free movement,
  so heroes can steer and attack in the air. New reactions: hurt front/back, knockdown
  and get-up (untouchable while getting up).
- Move data lives in dist/3d/moves.mjs, shared by the simulation and the clips, so the
  hit frames and the animation always line up.

CONTROLS (Dynasty Warriors style)
- J builds each hero's normal string; pressing during a move queues the next hit.
- K right after hit N gives that hero's finisher; holding K from standing still charges
  the old power move (packed snowball, Crescent cleave, Timber quake).
- J in the air is a jump attack; J after sprinting is a dash attack.
- L (controller default: right trigger, touch: Special button) spends a full gold Special
  meter that fills as you land hits. The hero is untouchable during it.
- Q (controller default: left trigger, touch: Paws / Scarf button) swaps Darren's weapon.
- Shift as Tormund while standing still raises the shield (parry, see below).

DARREN (3 passes)
1. Movement: idle with breathing and tail sway, walk, bounding run, jump/fall/land,
   tucked dodge roll, hurts, knockdown/get-up, charge, interact and celebrate.
2. Bare Paws: 6-hit string (jab, cross, hop kick, tail-whip spin, wing buffet, somersault
   tail slam). Finishers: wing uppercut launcher, tail-sweep trip, snowball volley, wing
   gust, belly flop (stronger with each belly stage). Corkscrew jump attack, rolling
   tackle dash, Scarf-Star Blizzard special.
3. Polka Scarf (new weapon): his yellow scarf with pink dots becomes a long ribbon
   (dist/3d/ribbon.mjs, a physics chain from his collar into his right paw that cracks
   straight on each hit). The short scarf end is hidden while it is out. 6-hit lash
   string (lash, backhand, overhead crack, twirl, figure-eight, helicopter rise),
   finishers (launcher, spinning top, lasso yank and kick, ribbon dance, shockwave
   crack), the hold move becomes the Polka Snare that drags foes to him, spiral-drop
   jump attack, ribbon-slide dash, Polka Cyclone special that pulls a crowd in. Longer
   reach, lower damage per hit than the paws. The choice is saved.

TORMUND (3 passes)
1. Knight stance (shield turned forward, sword ready), knee-bending walk, shield-forward
   run, roll, hurts, knockdown/get-up.
2. 5-hit string (rising slash, backhand, shield bash, thrust, spinning cleave).
   Finishers: shield charge, launcher cut, guard-break overhead, sweeping arc, leaping
   cleave. Plunge jump attack, shield-ram dash, Golden Crescent special, new Crescent
   cleave animation.
3. Shield parry: dodge from a standstill raises the shield; a frontal hit in the first
   0.32 s is blocked, staggers nearby grunts and answers with a counter-cut. Dodge with
   the stick held still rolls. Sword trails, victory flourish, kneeling interact.

BUCKY (3 passes)
1. Mallet on his shoulder, rolling waddle and heavy run, big landing thump, belly-first
   hip-bump dodge hop, hurts, knockdown/get-up.
2. 4-hit armored string (side swing, backswing, overhead smash, double spin): small
   hits don't interrupt it. Finishers: belly bump, uppercut launcher, hammer-throw
   whirl, log splitter (a straight ground crack). Ground-pound jump attack, rolling
   ball dash, Timber Avalanche special, new Timber quake animation.
3. Mallet trails, camera thump on his heavy hits, victory flex, interact.

SMARTER AI (dist/3d/sim.mjs)
- Grunts: the nearest four press the attack (still at most three winding up at once);
  the rest circle in a loose ring waiting their turn. They back off from a charging hero,
  badly hurt gophers and crows run for a moment (camp guards don't), crows dive-bomb and
  hay bales roll-charge after a glowing telegraph, and all of them step out of their
  boss's marked attack. A hero's special leaves them dazed.
- Bosses: moves are weighted by distance and by how you fight (standing your ground or
  charging near them invites a slam or a volley; far away invites a charge), and the same
  move rarely comes twice. Below half health they become furious (banner): wind-ups 20%
  quicker but still always marked, less rest, and often two attacks back to back.
- Friends: walk over to foes near the hero, fight with their own string and a finisher
  every fourth swing, step out of boss warnings, and come back when you move on.
