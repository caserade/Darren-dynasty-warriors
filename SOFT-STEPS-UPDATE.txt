DARREN'S SNOWDAY SEARCH — SOFT STEPS (SITE VERSION 5)

Owner-private update to the existing Phaser 3.90 Paws & Snow game.
The red Shoyru design, thin limbs, smooth featureless torso, yellow scarf
with pink dots, eight-direction isometric world, bare-paws combo, snow kit,
Tormund, three camps, Ruffy story and old version-3 saves are preserved.

SMOOTHER MOTION
- Cookie size changes now ease through a real display weight. Combat bonuses
  still use the integer 0–3 weight stage and movement speed is unchanged.
- A small bounded torso spring settles after growth. Belly/scarf follow-through
  stays nonsexual and honors the existing soft-motion and reduced-motion choices.
- One reusable padded 96×104 hero texture replaces rebuilding a full 624-frame
  atlas whenever size changes. Up to eight small trail textures preserve the
  emitted pose rather than changing old afterimages to the current pose.
- Padding repairs clipped crests, paws, kicks and tails. The retained atlas
  utility also clips individual padded cells to prevent neighboring-frame bleed.
- Attack extension and effects now coincide with the real contact windows.
  Damage, cooldowns and active hit windows are unchanged.
- Dodge, hurt, special, throw and stationary snow-roll animations use their own
  clocks. Walking cadence follows distance traveled. Pause, dialogue and hitstop
  preserve simulation-owned growth state.
- Live reduced-motion changes update appearance without needing a reload.

GAMEPLAY / SAVE REPAIRS
- Ruffy could spawn inside the keep and permanently block the reunion. His
  cinematic spawn now requires a collision-valid, walkable route to Darren.
- Holding a paw combo resumes after heavy-hit recovery rather than becoming
  stuck until the attack key is released and pressed again.
- Tormund selects targets from his own location, with matching ground and clear
  line of sight. He can no longer strike from across the map.
- Continue remembers the actual last camp cleared, even out of order. The new
  optional lastCamp field stays compatible with old version-3 checkpoints.
- Collected growth rewards and appearance updates persist into the matching
  current camp checkpoint. Run identity and matching camp flags prevent a new
  game or Reset Size from rewriting an older run's save.
- KO milestones restore correctly, avoiding a duplicate milestone heal.
- Buffered dodge is no longer lost at a lower-frame-rate cooldown boundary
  or while hitstop freezes its cooldown.
- Officer greetings cannot block the defeat transition or appear after defeat.
- Controller menu presses are consumed once: A confirms; Start resumes. Those
  fixed menu controls remain available when combat buttons are remapped.
- Reset Size updates the paused appearance immediately. The charge HUD updates
  even when Snow power itself has not changed.

VERIFICATION
Gameplay VM regressions cover the issues above, legacy checkpoints, run/save
isolation, gradual weight, contact poses, action clocks and a successful saved
Ruffy reunion near the keep at 120, 60, 30 and 25 updates per second.
Native Canvas scene/API assembly checks cover four camera directions, gradual
growth, bounded texture use and 4,992 padded poses (all states, eight directions,
four weight stages, scarf on/off). New game-scene renders were inspected.

Current verification uses simulation and a native Canvas stand-in for Phaser
texture/display APIs. It is not browser/WebGL or physical controller testing.
The local Chromium process cannot launch in this execution environment due to
an OS socket restriction. Earlier browser-test claims in older notes refer to
the previous release, not this new pass. Samsung/Windows hardware performance,
Bluetooth behavior and physical controller testing remain unverified.

No new image generation, external services or API keys. This remains a private
fan-made browser prototype, not an Unreal project or a 3D engine migration.
