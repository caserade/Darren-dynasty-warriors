SNOWDAY — THE THREE FRIENDS
Non-enemy playable 3D build, October 7, 2026

Open /3d/ from the private game site, or use the new link on the pixel game's title screen.

Included
- Terrain height, trails, terraces, ramps and scenery placement converted from this game's pixel world.
- Supplied Darren, Tormund and Bucky models, repaired and rigged in Blender, with embedded textures.
- Eleven animation clips per character: idle, run, jump, dodge, three combo attacks, charge, charge attack, interact and celebrate.
- Darren's charged snowball, Tormund's wide sword cleave, and Bucky's ground shockwave.
- Third-person orbit camera, character switching, companions, collision, energy and three practice posts.
- Ruffy rescue exploration quest, Firewood for Mom quest, six collectible cookies, dialogue, map, local saving and sound effects.
- Keyboard, touch controls and standard gamepad mapping. Low detail disables shadows and lowers pixel density.
- Original pixel game and its version-3 saves preserved. The 3D game uses a separate version-1 local save.
- No enemies in the 3D build. Future enemies can be authored from simple shapes.

Controls
WASD / arrows: move. Drag or Z/X: orbit camera. Wheel: zoom. C: center camera.
1/2/3: choose Darren/Tormund/Bucky. J: combo. Hold K, then release: charge attack.
Space: jump. Shift: dodge. E: interact. Escape: pause.
Gamepad: left stick move, right stick camera, X combo, Y hold/release charge,
A jump, B dodge, RB interact, LB cycle hero, Start pause.
Touch: left joystick plus independent action buttons. Drag the view to orbit.
Return to the cottage from Pause if you need to regroup. Progress saves at interactions, cookies and pause.

Verification
- Blender 5.2.2: final native meshes have zero boundary edges, non-manifold edges, degenerate faces, or failed weight sums. Maximum four skin influences per vertex.
- Final triangles including equipment: Darren 11,476; Tormund 21,786; Bucky 20,206.
- Reimported GLB: 287 evaluated animation frames per character, 861 total; zero nonfinite vertices or missing images; eleven clips each.
- Browser: all 33 clips load; skin weights and exact loop endpoints checked. Keyboard switching, all three charge hits, pause cancellation, jump/landing, dialogue, save/reload, low detail, simulated controller/disconnection and multi-touch cancellation passed.
- Responsive layouts checked at 1440x900, 412x915 and 915x412 in desktop Edge/Chromium automation.
- Thirteen simulation checks, including full rescue and firewood trips using actual terrain collision routes, passed.
- Existing gameplay, firewood, phone and verify-scene regression checks passed. verify-scene is a native Canvas Phaser stand-in, not browser certification. Its temporary image paths were made Windows-compatible.

Limits
This is a playable non-enemy build, not certification for every device. Physical gamepads, iOS/Safari and low-end phones have not been tested. The authored animations are a first gameplay set; final enemy contact, damage, crowd performance and balance require the next stage. Model appearance was visually compared with the supplied/reference artwork; generated source geometry and baked textures remain the foundation.

No paid generation service or new asset credits were used for this build.
