DARREN'S RESTORED UNARMED STYLE
New adventures start with Bare Paws. Darren carries no weapon.
Tap or hold J / Z: jab, cross, kick, then a full spinning tail sweep.
K: a separate 360-degree tail sweep with a short cooldown.
Space / X / Shift: dodge, including a cancel out of an attack.
F or Paws <-> Snow: switch to Darren's signature snowball kit.
In Snow style, hold J for a packed throw. K grows a rolling snowball;
press K again to launch it. L / C unleashes Blizzard at 100 Snow power.

MOVE & CAMERA
WASD / arrows: screen-relative movement.
B / N: rotate the isometric camera by a quarter turn. Q / E: zoom.
P / Escape: pause, character appearance, music and controller settings.
Follow the stone paths and ramps between the raised hilltop camps.

CONTROLLER / TOUCH
Standard Xbox / PlayStation button positions:
Left stick or D-pad moves; right stick aims.
X / Square or RT / R2 attacks; A / Cross dodges.
B / Circle sweeps or rolls; Y / Triangle uses Blizzard.
View switches combat style; LB / RB rotates; Start / Options pauses.
Bindings are remappable in the pause screen. A / Cross confirms menus.
Phones and tablets show a movement stick and Combo, Sweep, Dodge and Burst.
Use the Paws <-> Snow button to change touch combat styles.
Touch controls return and the game pauses if a controller disconnects.
Physical Galaxy, tablet and controller hardware was not available for testing.

COOKIE WEIGHT GAIN
Every 3 cookies adds one modest belly-size stage, capped at 3 stages.
Each stage adds +2 kick, +4 tail-sweep and +5 snow-roll damage.
Movement speed stays the same. Belly motion and scarf follow-through scale
with size. Darren keeps thin limbs and a smooth, featureless cartoon chest.
Pause can disable growth, reset size, hide the scarf or disable soft motion.
Size, cookie count and combat style save when a camp is cleared.
Older version-3 saves still load; saves without a style keep Snowball mode.
Local test-build progress is separate from the hosted game's browser storage.
