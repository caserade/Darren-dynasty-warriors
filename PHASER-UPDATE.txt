DARREN’S SNOWDAY SEARCH — PHASER PIXEL UPDATE

Play: https://darren-snowday-adventure.gosplekaiju.chatgpt.site
Access remains owner-only. This is a browser game, not an Unreal project or a Windows installer.

WHAT CHANGED
- Actual Phaser 3.90 scene, WebGL/Canvas rendering, depth-sorted sprite objects,
  palette-built character atlases and a crisp low-resolution isometric world.
- Eight facing directions, with separate idle, walk, charge, throw, roll, dodge,
  hurt and blizzard frames. Waving wings, scarf follow-through, snow footprints,
  impact freeze, camera shake, particles and optional controller vibration.
- Screen-relative keyboard/analog movement, quick acceleration and braking,
  a 150 ms action buffer, controller stick deadzones and right-stick aiming.
- Keyboard, touch and browser gamepads share the same combat actions.
- Controller menus work on phone, tablet and PC. Remapping and connection status
  are under Pause > Controller settings. Touch controls return on disconnect.
- Every three cookies grows Darren's belly one of three modest stages, with
  +5 snow-roll damage per stage and no movement-speed penalty. Size persists
  at camp checkpoints. Pause can disable growth or reset size.
- Optional scarf-free appearance, gentle breathing and belly wobble. Darren's
  body remains smooth and featureless in all directions, without chest features.
  Appearance, animation preferences and controller bindings save on this device.
- The existing three camps, clues, Tormund recruitment, Ruffy rescue and local
  checkpoint format are retained. Old version-3 saves start at the base size.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows: move. J / Z / hold Snowball: throw or charge, release to throw.
K: start rolling; press again to launch. Space / X / Shift: dodge.
L / C: Blizzard at 100 Snow. B / N: quarter-turn camera. Q / E: zoom. P: pause.

Standard controller positions (Xbox / PlayStation):
Left stick or D-pad: move. Right stick: aim.
X / Square or RT / R2: hold and release a snowball.
A / Cross: dodge; B / Circle: roll; Y / Triangle: Blizzard.
LB / L1 and RB / R1: rotate camera. Start / Options: pause.
Menu: left stick or D-pad navigate, A / Cross confirm, Start / Options resume.

Pair Bluetooth or connect USB through your device, then press a controller
button with the game open and focused. Browser/controller compatibility and
vibration support vary. Buttons can be remapped; trigger throw remains an
alternate unless RT has been assigned to another action.

VALIDATION
Browser-tested keyboard movement at all four camera orientations, acceleration
and stopping, charged shots, buffered attacks, weight stages and saves, camp
and rescue transitions, and phone touch layout. Controller input is tested
with simulated standard gamepads through Phaser's GamepadPlugin; physical
Galaxy S25, Tab S9 FE, Windows controllers and Bluetooth pairing still need
on-device testing. No claim of hardware certification or fixed device FPS.
