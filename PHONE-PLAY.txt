PHONE PLAY — PRIVATE BROWSER UPDATE

Open the existing private Site on the phone and sign in with its owner account.
Both chapters support touch controls. Landscape gives more room, but portrait
remains supported. Title and Pause include phone instructions and an optional
Full screen button when the browser exposes fullscreen support. Refused
fullscreen requests show a message and do not block normal browser play.
No app store package, offline installation, or cross-device save sync is added.
Saves remain local to the browser/device where each chapter is played.

FIXES
- Removed touch-action:none from the page ancestors so long menus, art sheets,
  dialogue and the size sketchbook can scroll vertically. Game surfaces retain
  touch-action:none to prevent a movement/attack gesture scrolling the page.
- Each joystick/attack gesture owns its pointer; a second finger cannot steal
  the joystick or release another finger's attack. Lost capture/cancel resets
  safely. Normal release still throws a charged snowball exactly once.
- Actual touch enables the phone HUD even on hybrid/desktop-mode devices.
- Resize clears held controls; orientation changes also pause active play.
  Resume attempts to restart suspended audio without an unhandled rejection.
- Landscape thumb controls respect bottom safe-area insets; joystick knob
  travel is calculated from its actual rendered size. Menu targets are >=44px.
- Touch rendering is capped at 1280x800; desktop keeps the 1920x1080 cap.
  These are resolution budgets, not an FPS or hardware performance guarantee.

CHECKS
Tests phone.cjs cover concurrent touch ownership, canceled/lost gestures,
charged release, rotation pause and hybrid-device control restoration.
Existing gameplay.cjs, firewood.cjs and native Canvas verify-scene.cjs passed.
HTML asset paths/IDs checked. No live mobile browser, Samsung hardware or
physical controller test was possible; device performance remains unverified.
