FIREWOOD FOR MOM — CHAPTER TWO

Play from the title's Chapter 2 button, or after Ruffy's rescue. The title also
has Continue firewood trip when an unfinished chapter-two save exists.
Darren remains the player; Tormund and Bucky are automatic companions.

Story: Mom needs six dry bundles. Darren and Tormund meet Bucky, the chubby blue
Grarrl, by the bridge. Three fallen-wood sites have finite enemy groups (3,4,5).
Clear each group, stand near the pile and let Bucky bundle it. Each site gives
two bundles; return to Mom's cottage for the cocoa-and-hearth ending. Ruffy
follows along. The retained snow-prairie terrain and roads provide the route;
this is an additional quest in the existing valley, not a separate world map.

Tormund: authored walk poses, sword wind-up/contact/recovery, carrying and
celebration. His close shield guard reduces damage by 25% when unobstructed.
Bucky: chunky blue body, wooden mallet, wide knockback swing, gathering and
carrying poses. Companion damage occurs at the painted impact frame, once per
swing, with range, facing, ground-height and line-of-sight checks. Both have
bounded cooldowns and use the existing terrain navigation.

Art: 32 transparent painted character poses (8 actions, front/rear, two cast
members), mirrored into four facing sectors as the camera turns. This is a
2D sprite presentation, not rigged 3D or a continuous eight-direction atlas.
New cabin, log pile, tied bundle and stump art. Original approved turnarounds
and action sheets are viewable under Pause > Tormund & Bucky's artwork and
moves. Generated source art is in art-source/firewood; tools/pack-firewood.py
isolates alpha components and packs stable-foot padded atlases. No per-frame
texture allocation for these companions. At normal play, only compact HUD
and current objective remain visible.

State: firewood.js loads after isometric-world.js and before phaser-scene.js.
Chapter two uses darren-firewood-v1; chapter one's darren-snowday-v3 save and
schema are preserved. Each meeting/bundle/ending saves chapter two. Retry
restores collected wood and full health at home; remaining encounter groups
reset. Starting a new firewood trip replaces that chapter's save only.

Checks passed: tests/gameplay.cjs, tests/firewood.cjs, tools/verify-scene.cjs.
The full quest was simulated over walkable terrain with automatic companion
combat, six bundles and the home ending. Tests cover timed hits, no distant
hits, shield guard, separate saves, chapter defeat/retry, old rescue saves,
32 nonempty padded character frames and four camera rotations. Native Canvas
renders inspected and character scale corrected. Browser/WebGL and physical
phone/controller testing were unavailable; no device certification is claimed.

The Site remains owner-private. This remains the existing Phaser 3.90 browser
game and preserves Darren's art, combat, weight stages and compact HUD.
