CONTROLLER UPDATE

Shared browser Gamepad API controls cover both chapters, dialogue, menus,
Pause, settings and the size sketchbook. Native numeric axes and Phaser axis
wrappers are supported. A/confirm and B/back use standard button positions;
Start always pauses/resumes, including during dialogue. Right stick scrolls
long menus/artwork. D-pad and left-stick navigation repeat when held; sliders
accept left/right input. Programmatic focus has a visible gold outline.

The active controller remains selected while connected. Disconnect pauses
play and clears held inputs. Reconnection seeds held buttons to avoid an
accidental selection or resume. Touch remains available. Remapping supports
button swaps, persistent bindings, defaults, and Start/Escape cancellation.
Start is reserved as an always-available pause/menu control. LT zoom no longer
moves Darren through its D-pad chord. Audio resume rejection is handled.

Open the private game in the phone browser with its owner account. Pair a
browser-supported controller through Bluetooth, press/release a button, and
tap the screen once for audio. In-game title/Pause instructions list controls.
This remains a browser game; no native app or cross-device saves were added.

Verification: tests/controllers.cjs, gameplay.cjs, firewood.cjs, phone.cjs and
native Canvas verify-scene.cjs passed. Controller tests simulate standard
pads and Phaser axes; they do not certify real Android/iPhone controllers or
browser compatibility. No physical phone/controller test was available.
